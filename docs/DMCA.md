# DMCA and takedown policy

Kurenai is an open-source client. The project does not host, store, upload or distribute manga, manhua, manhwa, webtoons, comics or light novels. No content files are included in this repository or in release APKs.

Sources are third-party parsers that retrieve publicly available pages from sites operated by others. The project does not control those sites, their content or their availability.

## If you are a rights holder

Send a notice to the maintainers through the repository's private security/contact channel with:

1. Identification of the copyrighted work you believe is being infringed.
2. The exact source name, parser identifier or URL at issue.
3. Your contact details.
4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorised.
5. A statement that the information is accurate and that you are authorised to act for the rights holder.
6. Your physical or electronic signature.

## What we can do

Because Kurenai does not host content, the practical remedies are:

- Remove or disable a bundled parser in the next release.
- Add the source to a local/remote block list.
- Document the limitation so users understand content is not hosted by Kurenai.

We cannot remove content from a third-party site. Contact the operator of that site for hosting removals.

## Counter-notice

If you believe a parser was disabled in error, send a counter-notice with the same identification and a statement under penalty of perjury that the removal was mistaken or misidentified. We may restore the parser in a future release.

## Repeat sources

Sources subject to repeated valid notices may be removed permanently from the bundled registry.

## Good-faith operation

The maintainers respond to valid notices promptly. Abuse of this process, including knowingly false claims, may be reported to the relevant authorities.

This document is operational guidance, not legal advice. Consult a qualified lawyer for your jurisdiction.
